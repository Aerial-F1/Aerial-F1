Our Mission

Precision Racing At IHS Oud Metha

Aerial F1 is a student STEM racing team representing The Indian High School, Oud Metha. Participating in the premier F1 in Schools competition, our team engineers miniature compressed gas-powered cars while running a full corporate structure covering marketing, sponsorship, graphic design, CAD modeling, and manufacturing.
